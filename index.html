The Problem

A nurse walks 3 km a day just checking saline drips

India has 70,000+ hospitals — most relying entirely on manual IV monitoring.

👩‍⚕️

Nurse Overload

1 nurse manages 5–10 patients simultaneously, stretching attention thin across critical tasks.

🚶

Manual Checks

IV checked every 30–45 minutes by walking to bedside — inefficient and error-prone.

⚠️

Missed Alerts

Air embolism risk when saline runs dry unnoticed. Preventable patient harm every day.

Our Solution

SalinePro — Real-time. Always-on.

A sensor clipped to the IV drip set measures flow rate and remaining volume — and sends instant alerts to the nurse's phone before saline runs out.
